Lantern Lane

Privacy

Lantern Lane is a puzzle game from Jason Fuscellaro. This policy describes the iOS app and this page. It was last updated on October 7, 2026.

Progress stays on the device

The game stores your progress, settings, and purchases on this device. It does not create an account and does not send that save to a server we operate.

Purchases

The shop uses Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. Lantern Lane does not receive your card number. Twin Flame, Triple Wick, Quiet Market, and Opening Night are bought once. Gem packs can be bought again.

Advertising

The app requests ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect an IP address (which can estimate a coarse location), crash logs, performance data such as launch time, a device identifier such as the advertising identifier, advertisements you have seen, and interactions such as starting the app or watching an ad. Google may use that data for advertising and analytics, including sharing with other companies that show ads. Quiet Market removes the banner and the break ads. Rewarded ads stay optional because you choose to watch them.

On iOS, the app may ask permission to track so ads can be more relevant and less repetitive. You can refuse, and the game still plays. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use the advertising identifier to recognize your device across apps. You can change that choice later in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

What we do not do

Lantern Lane does not ask you to sign in, does not sell a contact list, and does not include chat, user-generated posts, or links out to the open web.
